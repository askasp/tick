Does this change keep the rules this repo wrote down for itself?

Read the CLAUDE.md at the root and the one in each directory the diff touches,
and every skill in .claude/skills whose description covers what the diff does.
Then look for:

- a line of the diff that does what a rule says never to do
- a step a skill says this kind of change needs, left out
- a convention the rules name, done another way

Quote the rule beside the line that breaks it. A rule nobody wrote down is not
this pass's business: leave taste to craft and bugs to defects.
